# Sound credits

Every sound file in this repo is listed here with its source and license.

**This copy ships with no recorded sound files.** Every sound is generated in code by `src/audio/synth.js`, so the game works with sound out of the box. Those sounds are placeholders. They can be replaced with licensed commercial sounds, or any sounds you have the right to use, without changing any code: add the files and point `sounds/default/manifest.json` at them (see [docs/add-a-sound-pack.md](../docs/add-a-sound-pack.md)).
