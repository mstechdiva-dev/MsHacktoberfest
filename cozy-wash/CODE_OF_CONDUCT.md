# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Cozy Wash to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a cozy laundromat.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues or pull requests that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
